A classic arrangement of fresh carnations with lush ruffled petals. Famed for their remarkable resilience and soft clove-like fragrance, carnations are a timeless choice for express gifts, anniversaries, and heartfelt gestures.
  • Trim Stems: Cut 1 inch off the base of each stem above a node at a 45-degree angle.
  • Vase Cleanliness: Ensure the vase is thoroughly cleaned and filled with cool water.
  • Remove Submerged Leaves: Strip off foliage that sits below the water level.
  • Keep Cool: Keep away from direct sunlight and ripening fruits to prolong bloom life.
